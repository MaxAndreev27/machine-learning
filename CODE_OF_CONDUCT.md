# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Please:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Focus on the work and its impact, not on individuals.
- Accept feedback gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the repository maintainer through a private GitHub message. Reports will be reviewed and handled as promptly and fairly as possible.

Contributors who violate this code may be removed from project spaces or have contributions declined.
